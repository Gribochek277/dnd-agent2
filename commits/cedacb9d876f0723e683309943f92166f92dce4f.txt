fix: auto-EndCombat when the player kills the last enemy (issue #46)
After the player's accepted AttackRoll/ApplyDamage killed the last enemy,
the fight hung in Combat with a DEAD enemy until the player typed
«бой окончен» — the outcome depended on the DM remembering EndCombat.

System guarantee (InputLoop):
- after either side's turn, an active combat with no living combatants on
  one side ends itself via the existing HandleCombatEnded path: Combat →
  Exploration, XP granted (GrantXp transition action), HP persisted
  (SyncPlayerCharacters), checkpoint save — mirroring the enemy-AI path
- HandleCombatEnded no longer takes the unused EnemyTurnResult parameter

Enforcement (TurnPassAdjudicator):
- a pass whose accepted attack/damage result reports
  'ALL ENEMIES DEFEATED' (CombatState's marker on the killing blow) but
  contains no accepted EndCombat is re-requested (RetryMechanical)
  instead of being accepted on the AttackRoll alone; once the bounded
  passes are exhausted the narration is accepted and the InputLoop
  fallback ends combat, so the player is never left stuck

Fixes #46
