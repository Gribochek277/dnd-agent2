fix(dm): loot grant is single-use per turn (ticket 06)
A live combat-goblin-basic trial issued EndCombat/GrantLoot twice in one
turn and the same gold was added again, and the narration described the
outcome twice. The two existing repeat guards miss it: the consecutive
streak (N = 3) and the pass-wide limit (M = 4) both need three/four
identical calls, and both reset on a pass boundary.

- New ITurnActionLedger/TurnActionLedger (Dnd.Core, DI singleton) records
  actions that must apply at most once per turn.
- ChatClientAgentStreamSource resets it once at the start of each turn,
  so it survives that turn's corrective passes ("including different
  passes") but not the next turn.
- GrantLoot claims each granted item name and each gold amount as
  separate case-insensitive (character, item) / (character, gold) keys,
  atomically, before touching the inventory. A repeat is rejected with a
  Rejected: result and nothing is added twice; even a grant the model
  splits into (item+gold), (item), (gold) calls is caught. Different
  grants and the same grant in a later turn still work.
- EndCombat needs no ledger entry: the transition to Exploration already
  rejects a repeat, and the ledger deliberately does not block ending a
  genuinely new fight in the same turn.

Coverage: tool tests (duplicate, split-subset, per-turn reset, different
grants, EndCombat repeat) and stream-source tests (ledger persists across
corrective passes, resets across turns). dotnet test Dnd.slnx green
(1644 tests). Live combat-goblin-basic x3 (deepseek-v4.1-flash,
external qwen3.8-flash grader): 3/3 PASS, matches-mechanical-outcome 5.
