feat(combat): Rage as tracked battle condition (Gitea #15)
- ActivateRage tool: bonus-action activation in combat, consumes a Rage use
  (2 per long rest at level 1, 5e level scaling), applies the Rage condition
  until end of combat; rejects when no uses left, already raging, or not a
  Barbarian. Use count persists on PlayerCharacter (RageUsesRemaining).
- AttackRoll: +2 Rage damage applied systemically for melee Strength attacks
  while raging (STR >= DEX, ranged weapons excluded).
- ApplyDamage/CombatState: resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing while
  raging (halved, rounds down); damageType param on ApplyDamage/AttackRoll
  tools and enemy AI attacks.
- Long rest restores Rage uses (RestService + RageTracker); /characters shows
  remaining uses (Rage: N/M). Combat ending with active Rage is handled by the
  existing UntilEndOfCombat condition cleanup.
- Unit tests: RageTrackerTests (uses/recovery), CombatStateTests (resistance,
  condition, snapshot), DmAgentToolsTests (tool activation, +2 bonus, rejection
  paths). dotnet build 0 errors, 556 tests green.
