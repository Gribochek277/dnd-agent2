Level-up narration walks the player through new class features (#40)
The DM previously only narrated a generic "ты достиг уровня, стал
сильнее" on a level-up and never taught what the character actually
gained (e.g. Reckless Attack / Danger Sense for a Barbarian at level 2).

- Collect the leveled-up characters (name, class, new level) on
  IGameStateContext.LevelUps from both the GrantMilestoneXp tool and the
  GrantXp combat transition action.
- Pass LevelUps through PromptContext; PromptBuilder emits a <level_up>
  block on the level-up turn naming who leveled and instructing the DM
  to perform the level-up ritual and walk the player through the new
  class features, spells, and HP/stat improvements from 5e knowledge.
- Add the matching level-up ritual guidance to the DM system prompt.
- Clear HasLevelUps/LevelUps after the level-up narration turn so later
  prompts are not polluted (DmAgent consumes the pending flag once).

Covered by a PromptBuilder unit test (block included when level-ups are
present, absent otherwise), a DM tool assertion on LevelUps population,
and a DmAgent integration test (block reaches the prompt + flag cleared
after the turn).
