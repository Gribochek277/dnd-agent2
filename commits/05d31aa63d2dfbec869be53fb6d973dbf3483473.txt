fix(characters): keep max HP intact when leveling up a wounded character (issue #43)
Normalize() used to derive MaxHp from the current Hp, so a level-up while
wounded permanently destroyed the character's max HP (4/14 -> 10/10 max).
MaxHp is a stat: enforce Hp <= MaxHp by clamping the current HP only, and
initialize MaxHp from Hp once when an import omits it.

Regression test: wounded 12/30 fighter leveling up keeps MaxHp 40 (was 22).
