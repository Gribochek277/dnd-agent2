fix(dm): a player's ability check never damages an enemy (ticket 05)
A social reply in combat (no attack) could resolve as AssignDC(Wisdom) ->
TriggerSave -> success -> ApplyDamage(enemy) with an invented cause ->
EndCombat + XP: talking won the fight. Damage to an enemy now comes only
from an attack or an explicit hazard in the fiction; an ability check or
saving throw resolves uncertainty and never reduces HP.

  (TriggerSave) and DmAgent resets it at the start of every turn.
- ApplyDamage rejects damage to an enemy while a player check is the
  provenance; an enemy's save against a player's spell is untouched.
- Prompt states the rule; ApplyDamage description warns the model.
