message:
fix: instrumental weapon after the attack verb no longer starts combat (issue #47)
'От злости бью рукоятью топора по стене' extracted the instrument
(рукоятью) as the attack target, so the system combat-start gate fired
and even resurrected the last declared encounter.

- ExtractAttackTarget rejects a word with an instrumental ending
  (-ом/-ем/-ой/-ей/-ью/-ами/-ями: топором, кулаком, дубиной, рукоятью)
  right after the attack verb — it names the weapon, not a target
- 'на' is now treated as an attack preposition and skipped
  ('нападаю на гоблина' extracted 'на' itself as the target before)
- 'ударю' added to the target verbs, so 'Ударю кулаком по столу' is
  exercised by the rejection instead of passing by missing verb coverage
- The peaceful-message invariant is unchanged: the fallback combat start
  only runs when the gate (non-null target) already passed

Fixes #47
