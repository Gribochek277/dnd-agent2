feat(dm): enforce world authority — player claims never become facts (#13)
System prompt now carries explicit world-authority rules (RU examples):
perceptual claims (я вижу монстра / там стоит X / я нашёл X) are reframed
into a search + system check (AssignDC -> TriggerSave, Perception/
Investigation) narrated by the roll result; non-perceptual claims
(я знаю его / это мой амулет / он отдаёт мне ключ) get a polite refusal
and an intent request; the DM never repeats player-claimed facts as
truth, free attempts are untouched.

Safety net: IWorldFactClaimDetector extracts claims from the raw player
input (threaded through ILlmClient/IAgentStreamSource/DmAgent) and
TurnPassAdjudicator gains TurnPassAction.RetryWorldFact — narration that
confirms a claimed fact without system backing (no accepted TriggerSave
for perceptual, no tool at all for non-perceptual) is re-requested once
(exactly one corrective iteration, bounded; skipped when a state-changing
tool already ran to avoid double-apply).
