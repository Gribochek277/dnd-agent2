fix(combat): persist combat HP to player characters on EndCombat
Combat HP lived only in the combatant list and was discarded when
CombatState.EndCombat() cleared it, so damage taken in battle never
reached PlayerCharacter. /characters showed full HP and /rest short
healed 0 HP, wasting Hit Dice.

Add ICombatHpSyncService (CombatHpSyncService): writes each player
combatant's current HP (clamped to [0, MaxHp]) back to the matching
PlayerCharacter, matched by name case-insensitively. Both combat-end
paths call it before EndCombat clears the combatants:
- EndCombatTool (DM tool path)
- InputLoop.HandleCombatEnded (enemy-AI path)

With HP persisted, RestService.ShortRest now heals real damage via
Hit Dice (capped at MaxHp) and LongRest restores fully; /characters
shows the current HP and the next combat starts from it.

Tests: CombatHpSyncServiceTests (sync, clamp, case-insensitivity,
enemy/no-op cases), RestServiceTests (short/long rest healing, cap,
min 1 HP per die, full-HP no-overflow), DmAgentToolsTests end-to-end
(EndCombat persists HP; HP carries into next combat; 0 HP persists),
InputLoopCombatTests integration (enemy-AI combat end persists HP).
