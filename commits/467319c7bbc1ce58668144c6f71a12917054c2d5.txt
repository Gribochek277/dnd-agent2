feat(cli): hide model reasoning by default, show on request (#143)
The thinking stream used to render unconditionally at every InputLoop call
site, so a cloud model's English chain-of-thought — with long runs of blank
lines — flooded the screen before the narration arrived.

The reasoning block is now hidden by default and shown on request:

- `--thinking` at startup seeds `Thinking:Enabled=true`
- `/thinking on|off` flips the toggle mid-session

When shown, runs of blank lines collapse to at most one separator (leading
blanks dropped) and the block stops at a 3000-character display budget with
a one-line notice pointing at the full record.

Hiding is display-only: the model still reasons and the full text still
lands in reports/llm-turns (the stream source records the reasoning segment
before `onThinking` fires). New `IThinkingDisplay` singleton gates
`DmStreamRenderer.WriteThinkingAsync`; tests cover the hidden default, the
enabled path, blank-line collapsing, the budget and the narration path.
