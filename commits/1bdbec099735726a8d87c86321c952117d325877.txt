feat(srd): SearchRules tool over SRD lookup + SRD monster catalog (issues #81, #82)
Replace the seeded RulesRetriever and the hard-coded MonsterStatsCatalog
with SRD-backed implementations:

- SearchRulesTool now queries IRulesLookup (category-aware output, spell/
  item/monster/condition/feature formatters) and answers unknown names
  with a 'Did you mean' hint from the fuzzy suggestion pass.
- New SrdMonsterStatsCatalog (Dnd.DM): stat blocks come from the bundled
  SRD dataset via IRulesLookup (monsters category). Name resolution:
  direct lookup → Cyrillic transliteration («Химера-гоблин») → longest
  phrase/keyword windows ("Giant Crab 1", "Sahuagin priest") → fuzzy
  suggestion («Глубинный сахуагин» → sahuagin). Unknown names keep the
  legacy default block (CR 1/4, HP 10, AC 12).
- MonsterStats extended with size/type/ability scores/alignment/
  languages/description; AttackBonus derived deterministically from
  best(STR,DEX) mod + proficiency-by-CR (official table).
- Deleted: RulesRetriever, IRulesRetriever, RuleEntry (seeded rules),
  MonsterStatsCatalog; DI updated (IRulesLookup in Infrastructure,
  SrdMonsterStatsCatalog in DM).
- IRulesLookup gained GetCategory for full-category consumers.
- Tests: MonsterStatsCatalogTests rewritten on a SQLite-backed mini
  dataset (same case matrix + SRD cases), new SearchRulesToolTests,
  DmAgentToolsTests/CombatStartServiceTests wired to the SRD catalog.
