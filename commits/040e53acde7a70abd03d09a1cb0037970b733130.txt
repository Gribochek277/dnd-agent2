feat(dnd): add AttackRoll tool so player attacks follow D&D 5e rules
The player's attacks are now resolved by the system exactly like the enemy AI's:
d20 + attack bonus vs the target's AC, natural 20 = critical hit (damage dice
doubled, modifier added once), natural 1 = automatic miss, and the damage dice
the model names (e.g. 1d8+2) are rolled and applied by the tool — the model no
longer picks a damage number.

- AttackRollTool: resolves the full attack (PC attack bonus from STR/DEX +
  proficiency, enemy bonus estimated from CR), applies the damage through
  CombatState and reports the combined result; absurd/garbage damage
  expressions fall back to 1d6.
- Register the tool in DmAgentToolProvider (tracker-wrapped like the rest).
- Reliability layer: MechanicalOutcomeClaimDetector treats an accepted
  AttackRoll as authorizing damage/victory narration only when its result
  actually reports damage dealt (a miss authorizes nothing); the tool is
  resolving for mandatory enforcement; prompts (system prompt, per-turn
  reminder, mechanical correction) teach the AttackRoll/ApplyDamage split.
- ToolEventFormatter renders one AttackRoll event as both «🎲 d20=…» and
  «⚔️ цель −N HP (cur/max)» when the attack hit, so acceptance criterion 1
  holds for every attack; the enemy AI's roll event keeps its dice line.
- NarrationSanitizer strips leaked AttackRoll plan text.
- Tests: AttackRoll hit/miss/crit/fumble/rejections/clamping, claim-detector
  authorization (hit vs miss), formatter lines, prompt budget recalibration.
